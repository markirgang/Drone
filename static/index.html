Pixhawk 4 Drone Command Center Operating Manual & Flight Guide

Complete Operational Instructions for Flight Controls, MAVLink Telemetry, Gemini AI Multimodal Voice Commands, and Autonomous Waypoints

Step 1

⚡ System Power On & Hardware Connections

  • 1. Battery Connection: Connect a fully charged 4S (14.8V) LiPo battery to the PM07 Power Board XT60 connector.
  • 2. Telemetry Serial: Verify the GPIO UART cable links Raspberry Pi Pin 8/10 (`/dev/ttyAMA0`) to Pixhawk 4 TELEM1.
  • 3. Start Web Control Center: Execute the Python server command in terminal:
    python3 server.py
  • 4. Web Interface: Open browser to http://localhost:1190 and verify MAVLink Online status.
Step 2

🎮 3D Flight View & Manual Navigation

  • • Safety Arming: Click ⚡ ARM MOTORS to arm PX4 safety systems before takeoff. Click ⛔ DISARM for immediate engine cut off.
  • • Autonomous Flight Actions: Set desired takeoff altitude (1.0m - 20.0m) and click 🛫 AUTONOMOUS TAKEOFF, 🛬 LAND, or 🏠 RETURN HOME (RTL).
  • • Keyboard Nudge Controls: Use W/S for Pitch, A/D for Roll strafe, Q/E for Yaw rotation, and Up/Down arrows for climb rate.
Step 3

📡 MAVLink Serial Port & Telemetry Configuration

  • 1. Serial Port Selection: In ⚙️ Hardware & Serial, choose /dev/ttyAMA0 (GPIO UART) or /dev/ttyACM0 (USB).
  • 2. Baud Rate Setting: Select 921600 Baud for PX4 high-speed companion computer link, or 57600 for radio links.
  • 3. Telemetry HUD: Monitor live pitch/roll attitude, groundspeed, climb rate, 3D GPS Fix (satellite count), and battery voltage (16.8V max).
Step 4

🤖 Gemini AI Multimodal Agent & Voice Command

  • 1. Gemini Key Setup: Enter your Google Gemini API Key in the AI panel or under Settings.
  • 2. Hands-Free Voice Control: Click 🎙️ Hands-Free Voice to activate real-time speech recognition. Speak flight commands like "Take off to 3 meters" or "Return to home".
  • 3. Camera Vision Scene Description: Click 👁️ Describe Sight to capture eMeet Pixy webcam snapshots for live AI computer vision descriptions.
Step 5

📍 Autonomous Waypoint Mission Planning

  • 1. Mission Planner Tab: Switch to 📍 Mission Planner view.
  • 2. Add Navigation Targets: Click ➕ Add Waypoint Target to configure GPS target points and altitudes.
  • 3. Execute Offboard Mission: Click 🚀 UPLOAD & EXECUTE MISSION for autonomous GPS waypoint execution.
Step 6

🛡️ Pre-Flight Safeguards & Emergency Procedures

  • • Battery Voltage Protection: Never initiate flight if 4S LiPo battery drops below 14.0V.
  • • GPS Fix Threshold: Verify at least 10 satellites and 3D GPS Fix before attempting position hold (POSCTL).
  • • Emergency Return-To-Launch (RTL): Press RTL at any time during flight to trigger immediate autonomous return to home base.
